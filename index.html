Learn: UPI vs stock-trade settlement

UPI moves money between two bank accounts, usually within seconds. Stock-trade settlement is a separate process: after a trade is matched on an exchange, a clearing corporation arranges the transfer of shares and money between buyer and seller. In India, equity trades generally settle on the next working day (T+1). A UPI payment may fund a brokerage account, but it is not the same as settling a trade.
